Quest-drop database provenance and notices

The bundled Modules/Nameplates/QuestDropData.lua is a modified, ID-only
projection of pfQuest's Vanilla database. It contains no Wowhead overrides.
Native client objectives supply progress, completion and localized names.

Remaining provenance limit
VMaNGOS's database workflow imports world_full_14_june_2021.7z from
https://github.com/brotalnia/database before applying migrations. No
top-level license was found in that archive repository during the review;
license notices inside the archive were not verified. The database is
